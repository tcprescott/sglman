### New

- Tournaments can follow a start.gg event. Staff link it under Edit tournament → Integrations, and the pools, bracket and standings show on a bracket page that updates every couple of minutes.
- When your start.gg match is ready, you get a DM and it shows up on My Schedule so you can pick a time. Link your start.gg account on your Profile so we know which entrant is you.
- Staff can match start.gg entrants to members by hand, and can turn on reporting confirmed results back to start.gg for a tournament. It never overwrites a result already there.

### Improved

- Admin → Tournaments can sync a Challonge or start.gg bracket from the row's ⋮ menu, or every linked bracket at once from Actions.
