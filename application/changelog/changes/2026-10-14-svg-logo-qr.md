### New

- Staff can upload the community logo as an SVG in Admin → Settings → General, so it stays sharp in the header and the browser tab.
- Equipment QR codes can carry the community logo in black in the middle. Print QR labels has a "Logo in QR code" checkbox, on when the community has a logo, and each item's page shows its code with the logo too. The codes still scan at the smallest Avery label size.
