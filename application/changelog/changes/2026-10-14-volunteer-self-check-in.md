### New
- Volunteers can check themselves in. From 30 minutes before a shift, an **I'm here** banner shows at the top of every page and on the shift's card in My Shifts. Staff see the check on Admin → Vol. Schedule, as before.
- When a shift starts, the volunteer gets a DM with **I'm here**, **Running late** and **I can't make it**. Running late tells the coordinators roughly how late and keeps the shift; I can't make it asks for a reason and gives the shift back.
