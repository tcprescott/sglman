### New
- Platform admins have a new **Users** tab in Platform administration. It lists every account with its communities and how it signs in, and from each row's ⋮ menu you can set or change a password, remove one, or deactivate and reactivate the account.
- People without Discord can now get an account. **New user** creates one that signs in only with a password, and you add it to a community from that community's Admin → Users like anyone else. These accounts can do staff and desk work; playing in matches and crew signups still need Discord.
- Anyone with a password signs in at the `/password-login` page on your community's address. Nothing links to it, so share the address yourself. A wrong name or password always gets the same message, and changing or removing a password signs out every session that used the old one.
