### New

- Seeds now show their file-select code, the symbols players compare before a race to make sure they're on the same seed, as one line under the seed link on the schedule, the match details sheet, the Player tab and the seed DM. It covers A Link to the Past, Donkey Kong 64, Super Metroid Map Rando and DASH, plus Ocarina of Time when the community's OoTR key is allowed to read it. Final Fantasy, Zelda 1 and Wind Waker don't share their codes, so those seeds show no line.
