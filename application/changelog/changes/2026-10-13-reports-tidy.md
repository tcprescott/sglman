### Improved
- Reports use the same filter bar as the schedule boards. Dates, tournament and
  the other filters sit in one row on desktop and fold behind a **Filters**
  button on a phone, and a new **Jump to** row switches to the whole event or a
  single day in one tap.
- Every report block is now a titled panel with its CSV export in the panel
  header, and on desktop Capacity Forecast puts the busiest times beside the
  data they come from.
- Report charts use your community's colour instead of always being gold.
