### Improved
- Final Fantasy and Zelda 1 tournaments now roll from a seed preset like every other randomizer, so changing their flags no longer needs a code change. The SGL 2026 flags for both are built-in presets: import them from Admin → Presets and pick one on the tournament. Until a tournament has one, its seeds won't roll.
- The preset editor has its own field for each: paste a Final Fantasy flags link and check it on the randomizer's site, or paste Zelda 1 flags and see an example of the seed players get.
