### New
- A settings card with unsaved changes now has a Discard button, and leaving
  with unsaved changes asks whether to save, discard or keep editing.

### Improved
- Admin → Settings now opens on a page of tiles, one for each area (General,
  Event, Members and the rest), in place of the second menu column beside the
  side menu. Each area has an "All settings" link back, and the forms get the
  full width on desktop.
