### New

- Admin → Shift Board shows the whole volunteer day as a grid, a column per position and a row per hour, the same layout volunteers see on their Full Schedule. Tap a shift to see who's on it and whether they've checked in, are running late or haven't turned up yet, then assign someone, check them in, swap someone else in, move them to another shift or take them off. Tap an empty hour to add a shift there. Volunteer Shifts is still there for building the schedule in bulk.
