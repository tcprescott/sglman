### Fixed

- Syncing a Challonge bracket now drops entrants and matches that were removed on Challonge, so a reset bracket or a cleared roster shows up in Wizzrobe instead of the old entrants hanging around. Matches already on the schedule stay put.
- A Challonge tournament's Players list now follows the bracket: each sync adds every entrant linked or assigned to someone and removes anyone no longer on Challonge.
