### New

- Six more randomizers can roll seeds: AVIANART (A Link to the Past, door rando included), Super Metroid, SMZ3, Zelda 2, Super Mario Bros. 3 and Aria of Sorrow. AVIANART, Super Metroid and SMZ3 seeds show their file-select code.
- Admin → Presets → Import → From a randomizer… now lists AVIANART's published presets, and the built-in presets include the usual Super Metroid and SMZ3 settings.
