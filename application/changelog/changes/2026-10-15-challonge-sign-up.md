### New

- You can now sign up for a tournament that runs its bracket on Challonge straight from the Tournaments tab. Sign up shows the Challonge name you'll join as, then adds you to the Challonge bracket, and Challonge emails you an invite to confirm the entry is yours. You can withdraw from the same card until the bracket starts.
- If you haven't linked Challonge yet, the card's button links it for you and brings you back to finish signing up.

### Improved

- Challonge tournament cards now show who's on the bracket and how many entrants it has, including people added on Challonge by name.
- Admin → Challonge now tells staff when the Challonge connection needs reconnecting before players can sign up from Wizzrobe.
- Admin → Challonge remembers the Challonge communities you've connected with. Reconnect asks for all of them again, so you don't retype a community, and you can remove one you no longer use.
