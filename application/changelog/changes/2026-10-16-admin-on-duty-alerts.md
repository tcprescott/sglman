### New

- When a player comments on their match, the admin on duty and the tournament's admins get a DM that opens the match with its comments.
- Volunteer positions have a "Staff alerts while on shift" switch. Whoever's on that shift gets player comments and reschedule requests instead of every staff member, and if nobody's on shift, all staff still get them.
- Tournament admins can switch these alerts off for any tournament they run, under Profile → Notifications.
