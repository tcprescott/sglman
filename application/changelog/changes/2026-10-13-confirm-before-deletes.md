### Fixed
- Deleting or removing something now asks first, everywhere. Thirty-odd buttons
  used to act on one tap: deleting a qualifier (and every run in it), its pools,
  permalinks and live races; retiring or un-enrolling a bracket entrant and
  clearing seeds; taking a volunteer off a shift or clearing a draft; removing a
  tournament player; deleting racetime profiles, SpeedGaming links, webhooks,
  stations and role mappings; disconnecting Challonge or Discord; clearing a
  randomizer key, removing the logo or resetting colours; declining a join
  request; and withdrawing from a tournament.
- Deleting a qualifier now needs you to type "permanently delete", the same as
  deleting a tournament.
