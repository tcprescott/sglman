### Improved

- Admin → Tournaments fits on the screen now. It has six short columns (tournament and format, signups, seed, match length, players and a ⋮ menu) instead of a wide table you had to scroll sideways, and empty settings say "Not set" rather than leaving a blank cell. Descriptions are still in the editor, and you can bring the column back from the gear.
- Each tournament's ⋮ menu edits it, manages its players, opens its bracket or rules, and deletes it once it's inactive. Export CSV moved into the Actions menu.
- On a phone, each tournament is a card with the same four facts and the same ⋮ menu.
