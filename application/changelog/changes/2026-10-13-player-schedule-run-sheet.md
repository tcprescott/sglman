### Improved

- **Event › Schedule** is now one line per match, grouped into Live now, Up
  next and Waiting for players, so it fits a laptop screen and a phone card is a
  fraction of its old height. **Sign up** or **Withdraw** sits beside each crew
  role, **Acknowledge** beside a match you're playing, and the bell at the end
  of the line. Tap a match for its seed, stations and full crew list.
