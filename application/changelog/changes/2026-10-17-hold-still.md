### Improved

- Match boards (Proctor Station, the schedule boards and My Schedule) hold still while matches change. When you or another proctor checks a match in, starts it or deletes it, the row stays where it was with a small tag saying where it now belongs, or fades out as "Removed". A "Tidy up" pill at the top counts what's waiting; tap it, scroll, or leave the board alone for a minute and it re-sorts in one go.

### Fixed

- Pages no longer jump when a section reloads. Nearly forty lists and panels (My Schedule's crew and match sections, My Shifts, Admin → Users join requests, Check-in, Venue and Event settings, the Watch page tally and more) used to blink empty while they fetched fresh data, pushing everything below them up and back down. They now keep showing the old content until the new is ready.
