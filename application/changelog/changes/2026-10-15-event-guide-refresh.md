### Improved

- The event info pages now follow the organisers' event guide: room names and floors, opening hours for every room (Sunday closes at 6 PM), check-in desk times and where to find it, a table of on-site tournaments with their rules and sign-up deadlines, directions by plane, train and car, breakfast, food nearby and the nearest hospital.
- The tournament list on What's on, and when now comes straight from Admin → Tournaments, with each tournament's format, rules, bracket, sign-up close time and a Sign up button while entries are open.

### New

- Staff can tick **Test tournament** on a tournament to keep it off the event info tournament list. It shows a Test chip in Admin → Tournaments.
