### Improved
- Confirmations ("Delete this stage?", "Cancel this match?") now slide up from
  the bottom of a phone screen like every other dialog, instead of floating in
  the middle.
- Dialogs open at a size that fits what's in them: short forms and
  confirmations are narrower, and the bracket results, bracket manager and
  webhook deliveries open wide enough for their tables.
- Dates and times sit side by side in one row in the shift, tournament,
  qualifier, reschedule and export forms.
- Dialogs no longer show a second Close button at the bottom when the one in
  the title already does the job.

### Fixed
- A volunteer's profile on the Volunteer Roster no longer scrolls sideways on a
  phone.
