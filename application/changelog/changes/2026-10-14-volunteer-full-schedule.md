### New
- Volunteers have a new **Full Schedule** tab under Volunteer. It shows every role's shifts for a day as a grid, one column per role with the hours down the side, so you can see who's at which station and when. Your own shifts are outlined, and a line marks the current time during the event. On a phone the hours stay pinned while you swipe through the roles.
- On Full Schedule, **Columns** lets you hide roles you don't need and put the rest in your own order, by dragging or with the arrows. Your choice follows you to every device.
