- Staff and proctors can roll Wind Waker Randomizer seeds from the schedule.
  Players get a permalink to paste into their own copy of the randomizer, and
  the DM names the exact build it needs.

  Presets tab shows its build and main settings so you can check it's the
  right one. The SGL 2026 permalink is one of the built-in presets.
- Seeds you paste rather than open (Wind Waker permalinks, Zelda 1 seeds and
  flags) now have a copy button on the schedule, the Player tab and the
  proctor board.
- Zelda 1 Randomizer seed DMs now arrive as a card with the seed and flags
  ready to copy. Discord was turning the old version away.
