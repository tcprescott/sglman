### New

- Notifications can reach you by email and Telegram as well as Discord. Under Profile → Notifications, add your email (one tap to use your Discord address, or type one and confirm a code) or connect Telegram without giving a phone number, and every DM gets copied there. Staff turn it on for the community under Admin → Features.
- "Send a test…" on Profile → Notifications sends a test to the places you pick, so you can check Discord, your devices, email and Telegram each actually reach you.
