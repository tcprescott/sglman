### New
- Matches have comments. Open a match on the Schedule board or Proctor Station and leave a note under your own name: **Private note** stays with staff and proctors, **Message players** goes to both players.
- Players can comment on their own matches from My Schedule, say to warn they'll be late. Staff read it; the opponent never does.
- Each match line on the Schedule board and Proctor Station shows how many player and staff comments it has.
- When staff message the players of your match you get a Discord DM with a button that opens the match. Turn it off under Profile → "Staff comments on your matches".
- Webhooks get `match.comment_added` and `match.comment_deleted` events, tagged with the kind of comment.
