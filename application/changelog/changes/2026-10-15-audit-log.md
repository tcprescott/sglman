### Improved

- The audit log reads in plain English. Each entry says what happened ("Put a match on a stage", "Changed a setting") with a one-line summary, and opening it shows who, when, and a before → after table instead of raw data. On a desktop the entry opens in a panel beside the list; on a phone it opens as a sheet.
- You can filter the audit log by area (Matches, People, Settings, Check-in and more), and search finds entries by what they say, so "reminder" or "stage" works. "More by" in an entry shows everything that person did.

### Fixed

- Opened entries on the audit log and the engagement telemetry report no longer show as an unreadable white box in dark mode.
