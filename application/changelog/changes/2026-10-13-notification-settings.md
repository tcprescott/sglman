### Improved
- Profile → Notifications is reorganised into **Where it goes** and **What you
  hear about**. Discord DMs and each phone or browser now have their own switch,
  so you can turn Discord DMs off and still get notifications on your phone.
- The device switch shows whether this phone or browser is already on, and the
  page warns you when nothing can reach you.
- Tournament alert levels have clearer names: Not following, Streamed matches,
  Streamed plus possible streams, and Every match. The list is always open now.

### Fixed
- Turning Discord DMs off now stops every Discord DM, including reschedule
  requests and answers to things you asked for. It used to let some of those
  through while saying nothing would be sent.
