### Improved

- The availability bar on My Availability and the player schedule no longer relies on red and green: Preferred is now blue with a star, Available an outlined tint with a check, and Unavailable hatched red with a cross. Wide stretches spell the state out too.
- Volunteer shift cards write each person's state after their name ("Draft", "Acknowledged", "Waiting") instead of only colouring the chip, so it's readable on a phone and for colourblind staff.
- Report charts give every series its own pattern as well as its colour, so stacked bars stay readable for colourblind staff.
