  cleared. They're told it's cancelled, and they won't get a reminder if staff
  list it again later.

  shows your reminder beside the RSVP button, so you can switch it off.

  the event takes RSVPs, instead of always talking about reminders.
- The Community Events tile in Admin → Settings now mentions the staff approval
  switch it holds, not just the reminder timing.
