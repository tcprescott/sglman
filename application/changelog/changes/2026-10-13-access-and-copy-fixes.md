### Fixed
- Preset managers, sync admins and qualifier admins now get the Admin link in
  the menu, and it opens straight onto the tabs their role runs. A volunteer
  coordinator or equipment manager no longer gets a link to an empty Admin page
  while their feature is switched off.
- Preset managers can read the "Seed presets and test rolls" help article.
- Volunteer coordinators can open the Volunteer Coverage report from Admin →
  Reports without needing staff.
- Unticking your ADA accommodation request on Profile now asks before it
  withdraws the request and clears your details. Keep it and the box stays
  ticked.
- The privacy note on ADA requests now says what proctors actually see: an
  "ADA:" line on their run sheets and a button in the match's Ready to play?
  checklist, never your notes.
- The volunteer export no longer promises opt-in notes that aren't in it.
- The preset Test roll dialog has one Close button, in its title bar.
- "Open in Schedule" on the stage timeline only shows for people who can open
  the Schedule board.
