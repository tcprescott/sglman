### Improved
- On a wide screen, On Air puts each stage in its own column side by side, so
  you can see every stage's day at once. On a phone the stages still run one
  after another.
- Brackets, Community Events, Tournaments, My Shifts and Admin → Qualifiers show
  their cards two or three across on a wide screen instead of one long column
  of stretched cards. What's new shows each week as a card, two across.
- My Availability puts your windows beside the availability chart on a wide
  screen.
- Admin → Brackets keeps the tournament picker, Create stage and refresh on one
  row.
