### New

- Players in a Challonge bracket now get a Discord message when their match is ready to schedule, the same one native brackets send. Its **Pick a time** button opens the time picker for that match.
- My Schedule shows a count of the bracket matches waiting on you to pick a time, in the sidebar and in the bottom bar on a phone.

### Improved

- Challonge and Wizzrobe bracket matches now share one "Waiting on you to pick a time" card. Challonge matches carry a Challonge tag, losers rounds read "Losers round 2" instead of "Round -2", and a match that can't be booked yet says why under it.
