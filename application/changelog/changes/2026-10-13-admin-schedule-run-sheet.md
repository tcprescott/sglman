### New

- A new Needs you list sits beside the board (above it on a phone) with
  reschedule requests, results to confirm and matches running late. Approve a
  move, confirm a result or check a match in right from the list.

### Improved

- Admin → Schedule is now one line per match, grouped into Needs a decision,
  Live now, Up next and Confirmed, on phones and computers alike. Tap a line to
  open the match: check in, start, finish or confirm it, and set its stage,
  seed, stations and crew, all in one place.
