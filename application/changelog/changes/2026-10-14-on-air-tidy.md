### Improved
- **On Air** is tidier. The day arrows, **Today** and a calendar button now sit
  in one even row with refresh and help, the **Watch** button has room to
  breathe, and each match reads time and status, then players, then
  tournament and crew.

### Fixed
- **Open in Schedule** on an On Air match now lives in the match's ⋮ menu and
  only shows when the admin Schedule board can actually open that match.
  Equipment managers and volunteer coordinators used to get the link and land
  on a different admin tab, and tournament admins got it for other
  tournaments' matches and landed on an empty board.
