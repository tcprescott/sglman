### New
- Pin the places you use most to the top of the side menu. Tap **Edit pins** to pin, unpin or reorder them. Everyone starts with Event and My Schedule, and staff also get Admin Schedule.
- On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the screen now shows your first four pins plus **Menu**, on every page.

### Improved
- The side menu opens each area in place, so you can reach any Admin, Volunteer or Home section from any page. Admin groups its sections under Operations, Online play, Community and System, and the menu remembers what you left open.
- The admin **Schedule** tab is now called **Admin Schedule**, so it's not confused with the Event schedule. Its address hasn't changed.
