Volunteering is the shift-based work that keeps the event running. It is separate
from [crew](/help/crew), which is per match rather than per shift.

Your shifts are on **Home → My Schedule**. Everything else lives under
**Volunteer**. If you cannot see it, you have not been given a volunteer role
yet, so ask a coordinator. Some positions hand
you the role they need (Proctor, say, or Check-in Desk) as soon as a coordinator
puts you on one of their shifts, so a new tab can turn up after you are
scheduled.

> Proctoring is its own job with its own tab and its own procedure. If you are
> running a room, read [Running matches as a proctor](/help/proctor).

## Tell them when you are free

:::snippet volunteer-availability
**My Availability** on the Volunteer tab is where you say when you can work. Each
window you add carries one of three answers:

| Window | Means |
|---|---|
| **Preferred** | You would like this slot. The auto-scheduler reaches for these first. |
| **Available** | You can work it. Taken after everyone's preferred windows. |
| **Unavailable** | You cannot work it. The auto-scheduler will not place you here at all. |

Your windows are grouped into one card per day. **Add a day** starts a new card,
**Add window** on a card adds another window that day, and nothing is kept until
you press **Save availability**. Where two windows overlap, Unavailable beats
Preferred beats Available.

**A blank schedule is "no preference stated", not "unavailable."** You are not
refusing anything. But by default the auto-scheduler only places people who
stated a window, so leaving it empty usually means it passes you over. A
coordinator can turn that behaviour off and let it fill outside stated
availability, in which case you are considered last, behind everyone who said
something.

The short version: an **Unavailable** window is the only way to rule a time out,
and stating your windows is what gets you the shifts you actually want.
:::

> There are two availability editors and they work in opposite directions. This
> one is when you can *work*, and a shift only reaches you inside a window you
> entered. The one on Home is when you *can't play*: you count as available for
> the whole event and block out the times you are not. Filling in one does not
> fill in the other.

## How shifts reach you

A coordinator builds the schedule, either by hand or with the auto-scheduler,
which fits people to shifts using the availability everyone entered. Whatever it
drafts, **the coordinator decides**: the auto-scheduler proposes and they sign
off. A drafted shift is not yours yet. You hear nothing about it, and it does not
show on My Schedule, until the coordinator publishes it.

It also weighs more than availability: it will not put you on two shifts at once,
and it respects a cap on how many hours anyone works. If a coordinator has
recorded which positions you are qualified for, it keeps you to those and puts
you ahead of unqualified people for them.

Once a shift is yours, the bot tells you in a Discord DM:

| DM | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Volunteer shift assigned** | A coordinator puts you on a shift. | Tap **Acknowledge** to say you'll cover it. |
| **Volunteer shift reminder** | Before it starts (an hour, unless your community set a different lead). | Nothing, if you're coming. If you can't, its **Can't make it** button opens that shift's give-back dialog. |
| **Your shift is starting** | When the shift starts, unless you've already said you're here. | Tap **I'm here** once you're at your post. **Running late** asks how late (about 10, about 20, or 30 minutes or more) and tells your coordinator, and you keep the shift. **I can't make it** asks for an optional reason and gives the shift back. |
| **Volunteer shift moved** | Its start or end time changes. | Tap **Acknowledge** again. Agreeing to the old time doesn't count for the new one. |
| **Volunteer shift removed** | A coordinator takes you off it. | Nothing. It has gone from My Schedule. |

If your DMs are off you still have the shift. My Schedule is the place to check.

## Your shifts

:::snippet my-shifts
Your shifts are on **Home → My Schedule** under **Your volunteer shifts**, next
to your matches and crew slots. They're grouped by day, soonest first, with times
in your local clock. Each row shows the position and the shift's label, when it
runs, and where it stands (**Needs your OK**, **Acknowledged**, **On now**,
**Arrived**).

The line under the heading is your hours: everything you are scheduled for this
event, past and upcoming, with overlapping shifts counted once. Where your
community comps volunteers by hours worked, it also says which level you have
cleared and how many more hours reach the next one.

A row's button is **Acknowledge** until you've pressed it, which does the same as
the button in the DM. From 30 minutes before a shift until it ends, **I'm here**
takes its place, and a banner with the same button shows at the top of every
page. Tap it when you reach your post so your coordinator knows you've arrived.
The row then says **Arrived** with the time.

The row's ⋮ menu has the rest:

- **Shift details**: what the position involves, any **For this shift** notes
  the coordinator left, and who else is on the same slot.
- **Undo check-in**, if you tapped **I'm here** by mistake. It works until the
  shift ends, unless a coordinator checked you in.
- **Can't make it…**, to give the shift back.

Shifts that have ended fold into **Finished shifts** at the bottom of the
section. Tap it to see them, newest first.
:::

## Full Schedule

:::snippet full-schedule
**Full Schedule** shows every role's shifts for one day, so you can see who is
at which station and when. Pick a day along the top; the page remembers it next
time.

Each role is a column and the hours run down the side. A shift is a block with
its times and the names on it, and a block outlined in the accent colour is
yours. During the event a line marks the current time. Shifts the coordinator
is still drafting don't show here until they are sent out, and a block reading
**Nobody yet** is a shift with no one on it.

On a phone the hour column stays put while you swipe sideways through the
roles. When a block has more names than fit, tap **+N more** for the full list.

**Columns** picks which roles you see and in what order. Untick a role to hide
it, and drag a row (or use the arrows) to move it. Your choice is saved to your
account, so it's the same on every device. A role added later shows up at the
end, and a dot on **Columns** means you've changed something.
:::

## Giving a shift back

:::snippet shift-release
If you cannot work a shift, give it back rather than not turning up. Press
**Can't make it…** in the shift's ⋮ menu on My Schedule, or **Can't make it** in the reminder DM. Your coordinator is told immediately and can look for cover.

You can add a reason. It is optional, and your coordinator sees it.

Close to the start time the app warns you that it is late notice. That warning is
honest about the limits: your coordinator will know, but there may not be time to
replace you. Release as early as you can.

The button goes away once you are checked in for the shift, or once it has
ended. At that point talk to your coordinator instead.
:::
