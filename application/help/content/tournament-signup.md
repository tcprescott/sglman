Signing up puts you in a tournament's player pool. That is what lets staff
schedule you into its matches — until you are in the pool, nobody can put you on
the board.

## Signing up

The **Tournaments** tab lists every tournament your community is running. Each
card shows its dates, a **Rules** link where staff have posted them, how many
people have signed up (tap the count to see who), and a **Sign up** button while
its signups are open. Some also carry **Randomizer notes**,
staff's notes on the settings its seeds use.

:::snippet tournament-signup
Sign up on the **Tournaments** tab to enter a tournament's player pool. Staff
schedule matches from that pool, so nothing gets scheduled for you until you are
in it. Following a tournament for match alerts is a separate setting on your
Profile — you can do either without the other.
:::

Tournaments are grouped by what you can do about them: the ones taking signups
come first, then the ones you are already in, then any that have been announced
but have not opened yet, then the ones that have closed.

## When signups close

Staff set a window per tournament. A card tells you which way it is about to
move: when signups open, or when they close. Some tournaments have no window at
all, and those stay open until staff make them inactive.

Once the window shuts, the Sign up button goes away. Ask staff if you still want
in, since they can add you at any point.

## Backing out

**Withdraw** on the same card, while signups are still open. It asks first:
**Stay in** keeps your place and **Withdraw** takes you out. After they close,
the card says *Signups have closed — ask staff if you need to drop out*.
Withdrawing is staff's call by then, because a bracket may already have been
seeded from the roster.

## Brackets on Challonge

Some tournaments run their bracket on Challonge. Those cards carry a
**Challonge** tag, and **Sign up** adds you to the Challonge bracket itself under
the Challonge account linked on your Profile. Before anything is sent, a short
sheet shows the bracket and the Challonge name you'll join as. Challonge then
emails that account an invite to confirm the entry is yours.

Haven't linked Challonge yet? The button reads **Link Challonge to sign up**. It
takes you through Challonge's sign-in and brings you back to the card to finish.

You can **Withdraw** from a Challonge bracket while signups are open and the
bracket hasn't started. Once it starts, Challonge locks the roster, so the card
says *Bracket started* and dropping out is staff's call.

If staff added you on Challonge directly, link your Challonge account under
**Connected accounts** on your Profile and the card shows you as signed up. Your
bracket matches show up on My Schedule ready for you to pick a time.
