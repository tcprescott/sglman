If you're hurt or unwell at an event, staff can call someone for you, as long as
you've told them who.

## Adding a contact

:::snippet emergency-contact
Open **Profile** and find the **Emergency contact** card. Fill in their **Name**
and **Phone**; **Relationship** is optional. It saves once both the name and the
phone are filled in.

Only this community's staff can open it, one person at a time, and every time
they do it goes in the audit log. The card shows you when staff last opened it
and who. Another community you belong to never sees it, so add one in each
community you'd like to have it.

**Remove contact** deletes it straight away. It's also deleted if you're removed
from the community.
:::
