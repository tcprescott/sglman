Attendees can add one emergency contact on their Profile.

## Opening a contact

When you need it, open
**Admin → Users**, find the person, and choose **⋮ → Emergency contact…**. The
dialog shows their contact's name, relationship and phone, with **Call** (on a
phone) and **Copy number**.

Opening it is logged straight away, with your name, the person and the time,
even when they haven't added a contact. The attendee sees on their Profile when
staff last opened it and who. Open it when you need to call someone, not to
check whether one is on file.

## What stays private

There's no list or export of contacts, and they're never sent anywhere outside
the app.
