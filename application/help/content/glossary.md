## A–C

**Accommodation**: something staff arrange so you can take part, such as
step-free access or extra time between matches. You ask for one from your
Profile. See [Asking for an accommodation](/help/accessibility).

**Acknowledge**: to tell staff you have seen something and accept it. Players
acknowledge a match they have been scheduled into; crew acknowledge an approved
slot. The button sometimes reads *Confirm I can cover this*; it does the same
thing. Acknowledging is not checking in.

**Ask to change**: the button under *Your actions* that asks staff to move or
call off one of your matches. It's a request, not a booking. Your requests and
staff's answers are listed under *Your change requests* on My Schedule.

**Asset**: one piece of loaner gear in the equipment register, with its own
number and QR code. Only where your community lends equipment. See [Borrowing equipment](/help/equipment).

**Bracket**: the structure that decides who plays whom. In a bracket-run
tournament, matchups come from the bracket rather than from match requests.

**Broadcast tech**: the people who run a stage: the setup, the capture and the
stream. A stage is theirs rather than a proctor's, and it runs to its own rules
rather than a tournament room's.

**Check out / check in (equipment)**: borrowing a piece of loaner gear and
recording its return. Anyone signed in can check gear out to themselves; only
equipment managers and staff check it back in. Nothing to do with checking in
for a match, or with collecting your badge at the check-in desk. See [Borrowing something](/help/equipment#borrowing-something).

**Check-in (a match)**: the moment a match is recorded as ready to begin, with
both players seated. It is per match, not per player, and a proctor or an admin
does it, not you. Not to be confused with *event check-in* or with checking
equipment in.

**Commentator**: crew who talk over a match on stream.

**Comp / comped**: let into the event without buying a badge, because of a role
(usually staff) or enough scheduled volunteer hours. See [Comped staff and volunteers](/help/check-in#comped-staff-and-volunteers).

**Confirm (a result)**: the staff act of making a recorded result official. It
moves a match from {state:Finished} to {state:Confirmed}. Distinct from a crew
member confirming they can cover a slot.

**Crew**: commentators and trackers together. Per match, and separate from
volunteering, which is per shift.

**Crew coordinator**: the person for a given tournament who approves its crew
signups, alongside staff and the tournament's admin.

## D–P

**Display name**: the name other people see next to your matches, on stream and
in crew lists. Set it on your Profile; left blank, your Discord username is used.

**Enrolled / opted in**: see *Signed up*.

**Equipment manager**: the person who runs the equipment desk, lending gear out
and checking it back in. Staff can do the same. See [Running the equipment desk](/help/equipment#running-the-equipment-desk).

**Event check-in**: arriving at the event and picking up your lanyard at the
check-in desk. Separate from a match's check-in. See [Checking in when you arrive](/help/check-in#checking-in-when-you-arrive).

**Generated Seed**: the column on the schedule that holds the link to a match's
seed once it has been rolled.

**Harder settings**: a tougher preset some tournaments offer beside their
standard one. A match only plays it when every player privately opts in.

**Join request**: how you ask to become a member of a community that doesn't
let people straight in. Staff approve or decline it, and you get a message
either way.

**Lanyard**: what the check-in desk hands you, one per person: Staff, Volunteer,
VIP, Base or Day Pass, whichever is the highest you qualify for. See [Checking in when you arrive](/help/check-in#checking-in-when-you-arrive).

**Member**: someone who belongs to a community. You need to be one to see its
schedule, sign up for its tournaments, or be scheduled. Signing in alone isn't
enough.

**Needs crew**: the switch above the schedule board that shows only matches
still short of approved crew. See [Signing up as crew](/help/crew#signing-up).

**Needs review**: the chip on a finished match a proctor flagged for an admin to
look at. Confirming the result clears it. See
[Running a match](/help/proctor#running-a-match).

**Offer for stream**: the button under *Your actions* that tells staff you'd be
happy for a match to be streamed. A hint, not a booking.

**On Air**: the Event tab view that lays one day out by stage. See
[On Air](/help/on-air).

**Overdue**: a match past its scheduled time with nobody checked in. The Proctor
Station board puts these first. See [The board](/help/proctor#the-board).

**Proctor**: a volunteer running matches in a tournament room. They seat
players at stations, start matches and record results. They do not
confirm results, reschedule matches, approve crew, or run matches played on a
stage. See
[Running matches as a proctor](/help/proctor).

**Profile reminder**: the red banner at the top of every page when your
community wants something on your Profile that's missing, such as pronouns or a
linked Challonge account. Its button takes you to the field.

## R–Z

**Race room**: the racetime.gg room an online match is played in. When it opens
you get a DM and a **Join the room** button at the top of My Schedule.

**Request Match**: the button on My Schedule that books a match yourself, in a
tournament that allows it.

**Retired (equipment)**: an item taken out of use. It stays in the register but
can't be borrowed. See [Running the equipment desk](/help/equipment#running-the-equipment-desk).

**Room seeds screen**: a shared screen in a tournament room showing the seed of
every match about to be played. It opens from a private link staff issue per
machine, is read-only, and drops a match once it starts. See [The room's seeds screen](/help/proctor#the-room-s-seeds-screen).

**Seed**: the randomised game the match is played on. When it's rolled you get a
*Seed ready* DM, and the link appears in the *Generated Seed* column on My
Schedule (the *Seed* column on the Proctor Station board).

**Signed up**: you are in a tournament's player pool, from the Tournaments tab or
through a linked Challonge bracket. You have to be signed up before you can be
scheduled in it. Also called *enrolled* or *opted in*.

**Stage**: the place a match is played and streamed from, run by the broadcast
techs rather than by a proctor. The board's *Stage* column and the admin
*Stages* list are the same set of places.

**Station**: the numbered physical seat you play at. It is assigned to you at
check-in, and it then shows next to your name on the schedule board.

**Stream candidate**: a match staff may stream but haven't put on a stage yet.
It carries a *candidate* chip on phone cards, and the *Streamed & Candidates*
match alert DMs you about these.

**Suggest a time**: the button in the scheduling dialogs that picks a slot
neither player has blocked out. It fills the form in; it doesn't book anything.

**Tournament admin**: the person who runs a particular tournament. They can do
most things staff can, but only within their own tournament.

**Tournament room**: a room of stations where matches are played off-stream,
run by a proctor. The counterpart to a stage, and the two run to different
rules, and setting those rules is the event's job, so look under **Event
Information** where your community publishes one.

**Tracker**: crew who run the item and progress tracking viewers see alongside
the game.

**Volunteer**: someone who works scheduled shifts. See
[Volunteering](/help/volunteering).

**Walk-up**: someone checked in at the desk without a Matcherino badge. Only
staff add them. See [Walk-ups](/help/check-in#walk-ups).

**Watch**: to subscribe to a match you are not in, and get DMed on every state
change. Not the same as the *Watch page*.

**Watch page**: the community's public page at `/live`. It needs no sign-in and
shows each stage's stream, what's on now and what's up next. See [The Watch page](/help/on-air#the-watch-page).

**Withdraw**: to take yourself off something you signed up for: a crew slot, a
tournament while its signups are open, or a change request staff haven't
answered yet.

**Your actions**: the cell on each of your matches on My Schedule that holds
everything you can do to it.

## Match states

The five values the *State* column shows:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| {state:Scheduled} | A time is booked. |
| {state:Checked In} | Players are seated and the match has been checked in. |
| {state:Started} | Under way. |
| {state:Finished} | A result is recorded but not yet official. |
| {state:Confirmed} | Staff have confirmed the result. It is official. |
