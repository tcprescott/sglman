## The Watch page

:::snippet watch-page
The [Watch page](/live) is where you follow the event online. It's Watch in the
sidebar. You don't need an account or a sign-in, so it's the link to share with
friends, put in a tweet or print on a QR code at the venue. Anyone who reaches
the community's join page without being a member gets a **Watch live** button
that goes there.

On the page you'll find:

- On stream today, with a card for each stage. The match on now is marked
  {chip:live:On now}, followed by the next three, plus the approved commentators
  and trackers.
- Coming up on stream, which lists every streamed match for the next week, by
  day.
- Tournaments, with each one's bracket and rules links and how far along it is.
- Get involved, which the community fills in.
:::

![The Watch page on a phone: three stage cards, each with a Watch stream button and a LIVE match under On now, two of them with the next match under Up next, then a list of brackets](/static/help/on-air/watch-page-phone.webp "The Watch page, signed out.")

The community is still picking a design, so the page comes in three layouts: one
long page, tabs, or the player with a rail beside it. You start on one at random.
Switch with the buttons under the page title, and use the thumbs to say which you
like. Your browser remembers both.

The page updates itself as matches change. Matches without a stage don't appear
here. You'll find those on the Schedule view of the Event tab.

If you can run the match on the admin Schedule board, its ⋮ menu has **Open in
Schedule**, which opens the board on just that match.

## Finding the stream

If a stage streams on Twitch, tap **Watch** on its card and the stream plays
right on the page, above the stage cards, with the current match beside it. That
stage's button changes to **Watching** while it plays. It starts muted because
browsers won't autoplay with sound, so unmute it in the player. Tap **Watch** on
another stage to switch over, or **Close player** to stop. On a phone the player
stays pinned to the top while you scroll. **Open on Twitch** takes you to the
channel, or to the Twitch app on a phone.

Nothing loads from Twitch until you tap **Watch**. Once it's playing, Twitch's
own player and cookies apply.

A stage that streams somewhere other than Twitch gets an **Open stream** link,
which opens in a new tab. If a stage has no link, it isn't being streamed.

## When it's busy

If a lot of people show up at once, staff can switch the page to a lighter
version, or it switches by itself. It looks the same and the player keeps going.
The difference is that it checks for changes once a minute instead of right away,
so a match can take up to a minute to move to On now.

## Watching a match

Watching a match is separate from the Watch page. It sends you a Discord DM
every time one particular match changes state, so you don't have to keep
anything open. It's meant for matches you're not in. Players and approved crew
hear about their own matches anyway.

[Reading the schedule board](/help/schedule-board#watching-a-match) shows how to
turn it on and off.

## Watching in person

Whether you can watch from the room a match is played in, and how you're expected
to behave there, is up to the event. Some events keep the tournament room to
players and crew and send everyone else to the stream. If your community
publishes its rules, they're under Event Guide in the sidebar.

Stages are different again. They belong to the broadcast techs, not a proctor,
and the techs decide who can be where. Do what they tell you.
