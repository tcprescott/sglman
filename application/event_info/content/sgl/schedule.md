## Your own times

Everything above is the shape of the event. Your own matches are on the
**Player** tab in the app, and the full board — every match, everyone's — is on
**Schedule**.

Both show times **in your local time**, not the venue's. If you are reading this
from another timezone before you travel, the times you see are already converted
for you.

A match you are in will also reach you as a Discord DM when it is scheduled,
moved or cancelled, so you do not need to keep refreshing the board. See
[Notifications and Discord DMs](/help/notifications) if nothing is arriving.

## Watching

The **On Air** tab is the stage timeline: what is on now and what is coming up.
[On Air and watching](/help/on-air) explains how to follow a match you are not
in, including from the room itself.
