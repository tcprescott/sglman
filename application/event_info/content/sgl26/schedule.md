## Your own times

Everything above is the shape of the event. **Your own matches are on the Player
tab**, and the full board — every match, everyone's — is on **Schedule**.

Both show times **in your local time**, not the venue's. If you are reading this
before you travel, the times you see are already converted for you.

A match you are in also reaches you as a Discord DM when it is scheduled, moved
or cancelled, so you do not need to keep refreshing the board. See
[Notifications and Discord DMs](/help/notifications) if nothing is arriving.

## Watching

Matches on stage go out on [twitch.tv/SpeedGaming](https://twitch.tv/SpeedGaming)
and [twitch.tv/SpeedGaming2](https://twitch.tv/SpeedGaming2).

In the app, the **On Air** tab is the stage timeline: what is on now and what is
coming up. [On Air and watching](/help/on-air) explains how to follow a match you
are not in, including from the room itself.
