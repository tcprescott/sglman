Development fixture content for the `default` tenant. Its purpose is to be
**invisible** to a signed-out visitor and to a signed-in user holding none of
`VOLUNTEER`, `PROCTOR` or `STAFF` — if you can see this without one of those
roles, the role filter is broken.

It also carries the two snippets the Proctor Station wires icons to, so those
icons open in a dev run rather than rendering nothing.

## Looking after the room

:::snippet proctor-room
Keep it quiet, help players get set up, and know what every cable you touch is
powering before you pull it. Spectators are welcome as long as they are not
disruptive, and asking one to leave is your call.
:::

## When things go wrong

:::snippet proctor-escalation
**Fetch the on-call admin in person.** A player missing, hardware failing
mid-match, or anything that questions a player's conduct is an admin call.

The one you can handle yourself: the players disagreeing on the result. Record
your best call and flag it for review.
:::

## Handing over your shift

:::snippet proctor-handover
A match still in play at the end of your shift transfers to the proctor taking
over. What makes that work is the brief: every live match and how far in,
anything flagged or unresolved, hardware trouble at any station, and who is due
next.

Hand over out loud, in the room, rather than by message.
:::
